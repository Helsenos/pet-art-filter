# Pet Art Filter

A helper app for **Metin2 Jintea**. Hover your pet arts and it reads them, filters them by the bonuses you want and highlights the good ones in your inventory and stash. Includes a player market (prices in KK) and official filter presets.

## Download

Grab the latest zip under [Releases](https://github.com/Helsenos/pet-art-filter/releases/latest), unzip it and run `Pet Art Filter.exe`.
The app tells you when a new version is out.
